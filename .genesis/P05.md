## Implemented behavior

Finished banking answers offer **Get help with this**, using that answer's product rather than a later chat selection. Customers choose General or an owned account/card/loan by friendly name; cards use actual last-four digits. Product reads are authenticated and paginated, with empty/unavailable states. UUIDs remain internal references. General has no resource. The exact normalized issue text is user-supplied, not a verified diagnosis or model-written assertion; markup renders as plain text.

Saving creates a pending immutable preview. Editing cancels the old row and creates a new version atomically. Owner, source question, session, version, hash, payload and expiry cannot be changed in place. Ownership is revalidated on reads and writes; a friendly response label is outside the immutable payload/hash. Only the current login's owned source can create/read its preview. Expiry uses database time, normally15minutes. Cancelled/expired previews require a new question. Opening/reloading restores saved state; polling does not replace unsaved text. Uncertain mutation acknowledgements block another write until refresh. Session guards discard delayed responses after logout.

The dedicated confirm endpoint validates the exact binding but returns503 for a valid preview. No case writer, case number, successful submission or financial mutation exists here.
